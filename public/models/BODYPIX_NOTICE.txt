bodypix-mobilenet-v1-100-s8.onnx

BodyPix 2.0 (MobileNetV1, multiplier 1.00, output stride 8) by Google, from TensorFlow.js
(https://github.com/tensorflow/tfjs-models/tree/master/body-pix), Apache License 2.0.
ONNX conversion by Keijiro Takahashi (npm package jp.keijiro.bodypix 4.0.0,
https://github.com/keijiro/BodyPixSentis), Apache License 2.0.

Modified for onnxruntime: the seven dilated convolutions' "SAME" automatic padding was replaced
by the equivalent explicit padding (onnxruntime does not support automatic padding together with
dilation). Constants were also removed from the graph inputs (they are initializers). Weights are unchanged.
